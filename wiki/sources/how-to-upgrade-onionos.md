It’s important to ensure that your devices are running the latest Operating System, ensuring you have the most recent security updates, features and games. [OnionOS](https://onionui.github.io/) is no different. If you’ve already got it installed follow this guide to upgrade your device. If you don’t have OnionOS installed on your [Miyoo device](https://droix.net/product-attribute/brands/miyoo/), take a look at our how-to guide [here](https://droix.net/knowledge-base/article/how-to-install-onionos-for-retro-handhelds).

First things first ensure that you are connected to Wi-Fi. This can be done by going to ‘Settings’ from the main menu, then to ‘Wi-Fi’. Once it is turned on and searching, connect to your network of choice.

![](https://droix.net/knowledge-base/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2023/05/Upgrade-OnionOS-1-1024x576.jpg)
![](https://droix.net/knowledge-base/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2023/05/Upgrade-OnionOS-2-1024x576.jpg)

When you’re connected to the internet you can head to ‘Apps’ on the main menu and then scroll down to ‘Onion OTA Update’ and click ‘A’.

![](https://droix.net/knowledge-base/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2023/05/Upgrade-OnionOS-3-1024x576.jpg)
![](https://droix.net/knowledge-base/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2023/05/Upgrade-OnionOS-4-1024x576.jpg)

This will load the upgrade program, it is a text-based interface. Press any key (not the power button) to start the upgrade process.

![](https://droix.net/knowledge-base/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2023/05/Upgrade-OnionOS-5-1024x576.jpg)

Select ‘Yes’ to begin downloading the new software.

![](https://droix.net/knowledge-base/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2023/05/Upgrade-OnionOS-6-1024x576.jpg)

You can watch the download progress as the percentage is updated on each line.

![](https://droix.net/knowledge-base/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2023/05/Upgrade-OnionOS-7-1024x576.jpg)

Click ‘Yes’ to apply for the upgrade.

![](https://droix.net/knowledge-base/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2023/05/Upgrade-OnionOS-8-1024x576.jpg)

The installation with begin, and it will load a graphical interface to show the progress.

![OnionOS update screen](https://droix.net/knowledge-base/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2023/05/Upgrade-OnionOS-10-1024x576.jpg)

OnionOS update screen

![This image has an empty alt attribute; its file name is Upgrade-OnionOS-11-1024x576.jpg](https://droix.net/knowledge-base/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2023/05/Upgrade-OnionOS-11-1024x576.jpg)

At the final stage of the installation, verify the emulators, apps and settings that are installed. Press ‘Select’ to go to the next page of the check.

![](https://droix.net/knowledge-base/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2023/05/Upgrade-OnionOS-13-1024x576.jpg)

The device will reboot one last time and then it is done. Congratulations you have just upgraded to the latest version of OnionOS!

![](https://droix.net/knowledge-base/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2023/05/Upgrade-OnionOS-14-1024x576.jpg)

Further information can be found on the [OnionOS Wiki here](https://github.com/OnionUI/Onion/wiki/).
