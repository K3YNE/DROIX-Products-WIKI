This short guide explains how to update the gamepad firmware on your GPD WIN Mini 2025 (8840U and HX 370/365 versions). Please **DO NOT** install this firmware on any other GPD WIN Mini models, as it could lead to joystick malfunctions or render the device inoperable.

| --- | --- | --- |
| **FIRMWARE** | **RELEASE NOTES** | **DOWNLOAD** |
| GPD WIN Mini 2025 Gamepad Firmware v1.24 | Changed the trigger method for the background to support press-and-hold operation. Updated the HID data reporting method to support third-party software in obtaining button status. Fixed controller calibration issues. It is recommended to update MotionAssistant​ accordingly. | [HERE](https://gofile.me/7AWvf/b8xxqjwRz) |
| GPD WIN Mini 2025 Gamepad Firmware v1.19 | Fixed an issue where the left joystick’s up, down, left, and right directions were reversed when simulating keyboard WASD input in controller mode. | [HERE](https://droidbox.sharepoint.com/:u:/s/Purchasing/EclmdVA-nJ1GijWm9aR3tlABc4ppGwQvlt6RiMWAILLUBA?e=QpiJMQ) |
| GPD WIN Mini 2025 Gamepad Firmware v1.18 | Fixed the issue where the L2 mouse could not accelerate, as well as the problem where the downward direction of the gamepad could not reach the maximum value in the testing software. | [HERE](https://droidbox.sharepoint.com/:u:/s/Purchasing/EeLbMi9vhmlKiEmBWBQzNSIBPr90UF8xrwZzQM8EBOnFfw?e=9zxveQ) |

Please note that while firmware updating is generally fine, there are risks. Please ensure you follow all steps correctly, identify that you are installing the correct firmware for your device, have a fully charged battery and also connected to a charger. Do not switch off the device unless instructed to. We do not accept responsibility if something goes wrong.

Start by downloading the file linked above and extracting its contents. You will find three files similar to the ones shown below. Keep in mind that the version numbers may differ from what you see.

![GPD WIN Mini 2025 gamepad firmware files](https://gpdstore.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/GPD-WIN-Mini-2025-gamepad-firmware-files.jpg)

GPD WIN Mini 2025 gamepad firmware files

Ensure that the **switch** located on the left side of the GPD WIN Mini 2025 is set to **gamepad mode**.

![GPD Win Mini 2025 gamepad mode](https://gpdstore.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/GPD-Win-Mini-2025-gamepad-mode-1200x675.jpg)

GPD Win Mini 2025 gamepad mode

Next, right-click on the **WIN MINI2025\_Vxxx.exe** file and select **Yes** when prompted. The software will launch, displaying a screen similar to the one below. **NOTE:** The version numbers may vary depending on the firmware update.

![GPD WIN Mini 2025 gamepad update before updating](https://gpdstore.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/GPD-WIN-Mini-2025-gamepad-update-before-updating-552x600.png)

GPD WIN Mini 2025 gamepad update before updating

If the current version number is lower than the update version number, click the **Update** button to start the update process. It should take only a few seconds, and once finished, a notification will appear.

![GPD WIN Mini 2025 gamepad update after updating](https://gpdstore.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/GPD-WIN-Mini-2025-gamepad-update-after-updating-546x600.jpg)

GPD WIN Mini 2025 gamepad update after updating

Your gamepad will be temporarily disabled until the system is restarted. Click the **OK** button to close the notification, then reboot your GPD WIN Mini 2025. After restarting, the gamepad will be re-enabled and ready to use.
