This guide covers how to perform a factory reset on the DROIX X3.

There are two ways to factory reset the DROIX X3. The first is a full reset via the Recovery Menu and the second is from within the operating system. We recommend doing a full reset via the Recovery Menu.

Both methods will wipe the contents of the device and reinstall the Operating System, you will need to install and/or set up any apps you had previously installed.

## Recovery Menu Reset

**Note:** You will need a tooth pick, paperclip or similar item to press the reset switch.

Disconnect the power supply from your DROIX X3. Using the tooth pick, locate the A/V port (as shown in the image below) on the DROIX X3 and gently insert the tooth pick. You will feel a button press, keep this button held down.

While the button is remained held down, connect the power supply and the blue LED will light up.

![DROIX X3 AV port](https://droix.net/knowledge-base/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2021/11/DroiX-X3-AV-port-300x169.jpg)

Keep the reset button held down until you see the **Recovery Menu**.

![](https://droix.net/knowledge-base/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2021/11/DroiX-X3-Recovery-Menu-1-300x169.png)

Using the remote control, press the Up/Down directional buttons on it to navigate the menu and highlight **Wipe Data / Factory Reset**, and press the **OK** button.

![](https://droix.net/knowledge-base/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2021/11/DroiX-X3-Recovery-Menu-2-300x169.png)

Press Down on the remote again and highlight **Yes** to confirm to wipe all user data. Press the **OK** button to proceed.

![](https://droix.net/knowledge-base/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2021/11/DroiX-X3-Recovery-Menu-3-300x169.png)

The reset process will now start. It may take up to a few minutes to complete. **DO NOT** switch off the device during this period.

![](https://droix.net/knowledge-base/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2021/11/DroiX-X3-Recovery-Menu-4-300x169.png)

Once the process has completed, you will be returned to the main Recovery Menu screen. Highlight **Reboot System Now** and press the **OK** button.

![](https://droix.net/knowledge-base/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2021/11/DroiX-X3-Recovery-Menu-1-300x169.png)

The device will now reboot.

![](https://droix.net/knowledge-base/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2021/11/DroiX-X3-Recovery-Menu-6-300x169.png)

During this first reboot, the process may take longer than usual to boot. This is perfectly normal as the device is installing the operating system.

![](https://droix.net/knowledge-base/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2021/11/DroiX-X3-Recovery-Menu-7-300x169.png)

Once the device is on the Welcome / Set up screen it is now ready to use.

## Android OS Factory Reset

From the main menu, navigate to the **Settings** icon and press **OK** on the remote

![](https://droix.net/knowledge-base/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2021/11/DroiX-X3-Factory-Reset-1-300x169.png)

Highlight **Device Preferences** and press **OK** on the remote

![](https://droix.net/knowledge-base/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2021/11/DroiX-X3-Factory-Reset-2-300x169.png)

Highlight **Reset** and press **OK** on the remote.

![](https://droix.net/knowledge-base/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2021/11/DroiX-X3-Factory-Reset-3-300x169.png)

Highlight **Reset** again and press **OK** on the remote.

![](https://droix.net/knowledge-base/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2021/11/DroiX-X3-Factory-Reset-4-300x169.png)

Highlight **Erase Everything** to confirm you wish to proceed, and press the **OK** button on the remote.

![](https://droix.net/knowledge-base/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2021/11/DroiX-X3-Factory-Reset-5-300x169.png)

The device will now perform a reset and reboot.

![](https://droix.net/knowledge-base/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2021/11/DroiX-X3-Factory-Reset-6-300x169.png)

Once the reboot process has completed you will see the Welcome / Set up screen and the device is now ready to use.
